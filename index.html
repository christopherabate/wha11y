Wha11y

I can spot the main barriers to digital accessibility

Accessibility testing means spotting barriers and understanding how they affect people.
The goal is simple: know what to look for, how to test it, and why it matters.

Why Wha11y?

The Wh comes from question words such as:

  • Who may have a problem?
  • What kind of problem?
  • Why is it a barrier?

A11y is a short form of accessibility. The 11 stands for the 11 letters between the a and the y.

  1. Structure
  2. Content
  3. Visuals
  4. Adaptability
  5. Interactions
  6. Feedback
