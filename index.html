Wha11y

I can spot the main barriers to digital accessibility

Accessibility testing means finding barriers and understanding how they affect people.

The goal is simple: know what to look for, how to test it, and why it matters.

Why “Wha11y”? The “Wh” comes from question words such as:

  • Who? Who may have a problem?
  • What? What kind of problem?
  • Why? Why is it a barrier?

a11y stands for the eleven letters between the “a” and the “y” in accessibility.

  1. Structure
  2. Content
  3. Visuals
  4. Adaptability
  5. Interactions
  6. Feedback
